This guide covers implementing responsible AI in contact centers through Amazon Connect's native guardrails, outlining AWS's eight dimensions of responsible AI (fairness, explainability, privacy, safety, and more) and seven core guardrail components including prompt attack detection, content filtering, denied topics, word filters, PII detection, contextual grounding, and blocked messaging. It includes case studies and configuration instructions for enterprise-scale deployment.
