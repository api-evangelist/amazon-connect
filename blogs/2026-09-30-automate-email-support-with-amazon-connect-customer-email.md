Email is one of the highest-volume support channels in most contact centers, and one of the most manual. Agents read long threads, search internal knowledge, and draft replies from scratch, which makes for slow first responses and inconsistent messaging. Amazon Connect Customer now provides native AI capabilities for the email channel.
